RevoSim: Scalable Multimodal Tactile Simulation for Dexterous Manipulation

Zhang, K.*, Xu, L.*, Wang, Z.*, Tan, J., Li, C., and Xu, R.

IROS 2026 Workshop on Sensors and Actuators for Dexterous Manipulation (talk and poster). * Equal contribution.

Abstract

RevoSim is a scalable, high-fidelity multimodal tactile simulator for the BrainCo Revo3 hand that jointly models 285 piezoresistive taxels and five visuotactile fingertips. It generates synchronized pressure, depth, RGB, and marker-displacement observations; on a single RTX 5090, its depth-conditioned marker-displacement configuration supports up to 4,096 parallel environments. In-hand manipulation and grasping policies are trained entirely in simulation, establishing a foundation for future sim-to-real transfer.

Grasping as Anchoring: Expanding Urban Perching with a Hybrid Gripper for Aerial Robots

Han, Z., Mo, B., Cheng, S., Wang, R., Wang, Z., Gao, J., Pham, H. T., Ho, V. A., and Hovakimyan, N.

IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), 2026

Abstract

This paper presents a hybrid aerial manipulation system that decouples a compliant grasping interface from a rigid anchoring load path. The system combines a coaxial eye-in-hand camera, visual servoing, and robust flight control to enable grasping of everyday objects, perching on heterogeneous pole geometries, and stop-sign perching through a graspable adapter.
