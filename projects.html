6-DOF Robotic Arm Trajectory Validation

Illini RoboMaster

Embedded Team Lead / Manipulator Integration
University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

This demo shows a 6-DOF robotic arm tracing a horizontal figure-eight trajectory as part of manipulator motion validation. I worked on the arm's electromechanical bring-up, joint-level testing, communication-path debugging, and the workflow used to exercise larger task-space motions after single-joint checks.

The broader arm system integrated actuator communication, embedded control, and motion-planning workflows used during iterative RoboMaster arm development. This trajectory demo is one compact view of the validation process: smooth multi-joint coordination, repeatable path execution, and practical debugging on real hardware.

Robot Dynamics and Control Final Project

Fall 2024

Course Project | ECE 489: Robot Dynamics and Control
University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

Built and validated a task-space controller in C for a CRS robotic manipulator, combining PD and impedance control with gravity and friction compensation.

  • Programmed a complete hardware task sequence spanning peg-in-hole insertion, constrained zigzag tracking, and controlled contact with a fragile object
  • Used frame-aligned, direction-dependent stiffness to balance trajectory accuracy with compliance along contact constraints
  • Tuned transitions between position and force control, maintaining the required 500-1000 gram contact-force range without damaging the object
