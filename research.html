Grasping as Anchoring: Expanding Urban Perching with a Hybrid Gripper for Aerial Robots

May 2025 - Sep 2025

Undergraduate Researcher | Advanced Control Research Lab
Advisor: Prof. Naira Hovakimyan | University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

Developed real-time vision and pose-estimation pipelines for autonomous aerial perching with a hybrid gripper, including pole perching and stop-sign perching through graspable anchoring adapters.

  • Developed real-time detection and pose-estimation pipelines from camera/sensor specifications, delivering target state estimates for onboard perching control
  • Designed and validated approach-trajectory and contact-dynamics controllers for repeatable perching maneuvers
  • Contributed to "Grasping as Anchoring: Expanding Urban Perching with a Hybrid Gripper for Aerial Robots," accepted to IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026

Whole-Body Bilateral Teleoperation with Multi-Stage Object Parameter Estimation for Wheeled Humanoid Locomanipulation

Aug 2024 - May 2025

Undergraduate Researcher | RoboDesign Lab
Advisor: Prof. Joao Ramos | University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

Contributed RGB-D perception and embedded sensing to an object-aware whole-body bilateral teleoperation framework for the wheeled humanoid SATYRR. The broader work was accepted for publication in IEEE Transactions on Robotics in 2026.

  • Deployed a RealSense and Jetson-based object-size estimator and evaluated its output against caliper measurements; buffering and outlier rejection improved accuracy by 15% on a benchmark object
  • Built the RGB-D processing path that supplies object dimensions to the system's multi-stage inertial parameter estimator
  • Supported the embedded sensing stack used in hardware experiments for lifting, delivering, and releasing heavy objects through whole-body teleoperation
